ABOUT GTH

About GTH

God’stime Holdings (GTH) is a connected digital ecosystem built around people, platforms, ideas, technology and opportunity.

GTH brings different digital platforms together under one wider ecosystem while allowing each platform to have its own identity, purpose and direction.

The ecosystem is designed to create a starting point where people can discover useful digital experiences, information, media, financial resources, software and opportunities.

GTH includes platforms such as God’stime Fund (GTF), GT Channels (GTC), GT Softwares (GTS) and GT Connect. Each serves a different area of the wider ecosystem.

GTH is also designed to remain flexible. As new ideas, projects and technologies develop, new platforms and experiences can become part of the ecosystem.

At its core, GTH is about building, connecting and growing useful digital experiences over time.
