NGX OVERVIEW

Nigerian Exchange

NGX provides a marketplace for securities including equities, exchange-traded products and bonds, together with market data and related information services.

NGX All-Share Index

The NGX All-Share Index tracks the general market movement of listed equities on Nigerian Exchange, including Growth Board companies regardless of capitalization.
