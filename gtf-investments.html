GTF REVIEW FRAMEWORK

How GTF Evaluates Opportunities

GTF evaluates investment opportunities and platforms using a structured research process. The review considers the investment company, the nature of the investment, available information, historical performance where applicable, fees, liquidity, risk and other important factors.

01. Company & Platform

We examine the investment company, its platform, available services and the information provided about its investment products.

02. Investment Objective

We consider what the investment is designed to achieve, the type of assets involved and the strategy used.

03. Risk & Liquidity

We consider the potential risks, volatility, liquidity conditions and other factors that may affect an investor.

04. Fees & Conditions

Relevant fees, charges, minimum investment requirements, withdrawal conditions and other terms are considered.

05. Available Information

We review available documentation, disclosures, regulatory information and other relevant material.
